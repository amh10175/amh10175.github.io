Step Five: Stick It

If you are working with a simple design, carefully peel the backing from the vinyl and place your sticker wherever you would like. For designs with multiple layers of vinyl, align each layer carefully before applying them together to create the final design. Take your time to make sure each piece is positioned correctly for a clean and professional result.

Quick Tip: I like to use a piece of tape as a guide where I need to stick my vinyl. It’s better to lay it right the first time to prevent stretching and tearing.
