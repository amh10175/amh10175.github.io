Step One: Choose a Design

The first step is deciding what sticker you want to create. Your design can be anything from a simple shape or logo to a more detailed and complex illustration. Start by sketching your idea on paper to plan the size, shape, and overall layout of your sticker. This drawing will serve as your guide for creating the final vinyl design.
